The part of Anthropic’s Forward Deployed Engineer job description that caught my attention was where the engineer works: inside the customer’s team. I’d been thinking about how quickly AI can help me build a prototype. This role made me think about what happens when that prototype has to fit into someone’s actual workday. [Anthropic’s FDE job description](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5391016008-23)

## What happens after the demo?

Say I build a tool that summarizes incoming customer requests. In a demo, I can show that the summaries are accurate. Once the support team tries it, I might learn that they spend most of their time deciding who should handle each request. A better summary helps a little. Knowing where the request should go might help a lot more.

That’s the part of the role I’m curious about. You get close enough to the work to find out where your first idea falls short, and you’re in a position to change what you build.

## What stood out about Anthropic’s training

Anthropic’s Claude Frontier Academy includes a residency that starts with a simulated enterprise deployment. Residents then spend 12 weeks leading a Claude deployment at their own organization. I’d be curious to see how their plans change once people start using what they build. That’s where I’d expect the most interesting lessons to come from. [Claude Frontier Academy](https://claude.com/programs/frontier-academy)
